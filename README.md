# The Hidden Abode

An interactive study guide and pass-and-play quiz game on **Karl Marx**, plus E. K. Hunt's commentary. Built for ECO 4343 (History of Economic Thought).

**Play it:** https://hyeung024.github.io/hidden-abode/

## What's inside

- **Play:** 2–4 players on one screen, 63 questions in 5 decks (Commodities, Surplus Value, Prices & Profit, Origins, Laws of Motion). Each turn you pick a deck, then choose a circuit: **C–M–C** (safe: +£100) or **M–C–M′** (risky: +£250 / −£100). After each round a **law of motion** hits the market (concentration, commercial crisis, foreign trade, or a struggle over the working day). At the end you get a list of every missed question to review.
- **Study:** nine one-card summaries of the chapter, plus a table of how Marx relates to Smith, Ricardo, Malthus, Say/Senior/Bastiat, Mill, and Hodgskin.
- **Workday Lab:** sliders for the working day, necessary labor, and constant capital. Watch s, s/v, c/v, and the rate of profit change.
- **Values → Prices:** Marx's five-industry transformation example (Hunt's Tables 9.1 and 9.2), before and after competition equalizes the profit rate.
- **Flashcards:** 25 key terms to flip and shuffle.

## Files

- `index.html`: the whole app in one file. Open it in any browser; no install needed.
- `Marx-Summary.md`: written study summary of the chapter.

Content is based on Hunt & Lautzenheiser, *History of Economic Thought: A Critical Perspective* (2011), ch. 9 (pp. 202–246). The book itself is not included.
